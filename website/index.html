GET STARTED

From download to development.

01

Choose a pack

Download and extract the ZIP. Read its individual instructions before executing scripts.

02

Preview and install

Python 3.9+ is required. Replace the path with your project directory.

python3 install.py /path/to/project
python3 install.py /path/to/project --apply
03

Use with your agent

Open the installed artifact or contract in your AI coding tool, follow the workflow and verify results. Most packs are guided templates, not autonomous services.
